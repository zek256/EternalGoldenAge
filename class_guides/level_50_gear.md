# Gearing Up at Level 50

You've hit the level cap. Your stats are as high as this life will take them, so from here your gear
is what makes you stronger. This guide shows how to go from "whatever I was wearing at 49" to a
full endgame set, class by class, in three steps:

1. **The Sunspire shopping trip.** Spend your gold on the best gear that can be bought.
2. **The world boss circuit.** Farm the 40-52 bosses and their areas for gear that's far better.
3. **The Planes of the Gods.** The mythic gear, once you're strong enough (usually after a rebirth or two).

Item levels and drop chances below come straight from the game data. Bosses respawn after 120 ticks
(about 8 minutes), everything else after 30.

## Before you start

- **Fill every slot.** An empty off-hand or a level-28 pair of gloves costs you more than a small
  upgrade anywhere else. Check `equipment` for gaps first.
- **Stack your class's stats.** Casters want their spell stat (INT or WIS) first. Fighters want
  their attack stat (STR or DEX) and weapon damage. Everyone wants CON for HP after that.
- **Rebirth doesn't throw gear away.** When you're reborn, gear above level 1 goes back into your
  pack, not into the void. Your next life can wear it again once it reaches the item's level, as long
  as it's trained for it (training from every life is kept). A good level-50 set is never wasted.
- **The Planes are hard on a first life.** Even the first-tier plane monsters beat a one-life
  character about half the time, and the champions are built for characters with several lives.
  Finish steps 1 and 2 before you go up the Stair of Dawn.

## Step 1: the Sunspire shopping trip

Sunspire, the golden city in the Scorchsands, has the best shops in the world:

- **Zahir the Bladewright** (the Street of Smiths) sells the **sunsteel** mail and plate sets, the
  **sandrunner** leather set and **sand-strider boots**, the **crescent greatsword**, the **sunsteel
  scimitar**, the **dune recurve bow** and the **sunburst shield**.
- **Amsa Silkwind** (the Silk Bazaar) sells the **silkwind** cloth set (turban, gloves, trousers,
  slippers), the **desert silk robe**, the **sunfire staff**, the **sunlit codex**, the **amulet of
  the obelisk**, the **oasis pearl ring** and the **sunstone ring**.

Everything there is level 38-44. It won't be your final gear, but it fills holes cheaply and gets
you ready for the bosses.

## Step 2: the world boss circuit

| Where | What to kill | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Tomb of the Sand Kings** (below the sunken pyramid, north of Sunspire) | **Ussreth** (L48) | The Scepter of the Sand Kings (50%), the Khopesh of Ussreth (50%), the Burial Mask (60%) |
| **Thornwick Hall** (west of the Eldergrove's Stag Walk) | **Lord Aldric Vane** (L50) and his household | The Vane longsword (50%), the Vane heirloom plate (50%), the Vane signet (60%), plus the family and staff (see each class) |
| **The Ashen Peaks** and the Dragon's Lair | **Pyraxis** (L52), plus flamecallers, flame demons, magma titans, ash drakes, obsidian golems and dragonkin guards | The Fang of Pyraxis (70%), Dragonscale Mail (70%), the Heart of Flame (50%); rare drops such as the dragonbone staff and obsidian greatblade |
| **Ruins of Elandor** | **Seluvar** (L41) | The Ring of the Archmage (60%) and Seluvar's Hollow Wand (60%) for casters |

**Pyraxis is the big one.** He's the hardest boss before the planes, and three of the best items in
the world drop from him. Go in with full HP and mana and plenty of potions, and don't try him until
the rest of your set is in place. **Ussreth and Lord Vane** are much more manageable at 50.

## Step 3: the Planes of the Gods

The Planes (levels 51-60, up the Stair of Dawn behind Brightwater's temple) hold the mythic gear.
Each champion and elemental lord has a 40% chance of dropping each of their items, and Lord Trepth
has 50%:

| Boss | Where | Drops |
|---|---|---|
| Caelestra | Plane of Stars | **Stormcaller** (bow), **starweave robes** (cloth) |
| Somnara | Plane of Dreams | **the Dreamweaver's Staff**, **dreamsilver greaves** (plate) |
| Morthis | Plane of Death | **Gravesong** (sword), **the Death Mask** (leather) |
| Stalworth Lightbearer | Plane of Law | **the Lightbearer's Oath** (sword), **the Shield of Judgement** |
| Rekkn | Plane of Fire | **Flameheart** (blunt), **gauntlets of the pyre** (plate) |
| Nerevos | Plane of Water | **Tidecaller** (two-handed), **the Pearl of the Deep** (ring) |
| Ulmathra | Plane of Earth | **the Mountainheart Shield**, **stonestride boots** (plate) |
| Zephyrion | Plane of Air | **windwalker boots** (leather), **the Band of the Four Winds** (ring) |
| Lord Trepth | Plane of Eternity | **Eternity** (any weapon), **the Crown of Trepth**, **the Hourglass Amulet** |

Remember the gates: each elemental plane needs its first-tier champion dead, and the Door of Eternity
needs all four elemental lords.

## Class by class

Each table reads left to right: what to buy, then what to farm before the planes, then the mythic

### Necromancer

INT first, then CON. The same cloth, staves, wands and daggers as the wizard, so the wizard's table
applies to you in full. Your pet's strength is fixed when you raise it, so raise a fresh skeleton
or colossus after every upgrade.

**Priority:** the same as the wizard, with one difference: your pet and drains make the bosses
easier, so you can take on Pyraxis earlier.

## A suggested route at 50

1. **Sunspire:** buy every shop piece that beats what you're wearing, especially empty slots.
2. **Tomb of the Sand Kings:** kill Ussreth until his drop for your class lands.
3. **Thornwick Hall:** clear the house top to bottom. Nearly every class has something here, and
   Lord Vane drops a sword, a breastplate and a ring.
4. **Ashen Peaks:** farm the dragon cult and the drakes on the way up, then Pyraxis when you're ready.
5. **Then decide:** rebirth into a new class (your gear waits in your pack), or try the Planes.
